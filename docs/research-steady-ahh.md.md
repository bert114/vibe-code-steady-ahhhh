Proposed Research Plan

Research Areas:

Problem & User Validation — Investigate whether loneliness, emotional drain, difficulty identifying emotional triggers, and boundary-setting are meaningful problems for users, and what evidence exists that people would use an app like this.
Successful Wellness App & Competitor Research — Find existing wellness, mood-tracking, journaling, mental-wellness, and emotional self-awareness apps that have demonstrated success. Compare their features, user experience, target users, pricing/business model, and user feedback to identify useful patterns and gaps.
AI-Powered Personal Insights — Research how AI can safely analyze a user's own wellness data and turn it into understandable patterns, such as recurring triggers, emotional trends, draining interactions, and possible boundary concerns. The research should also examine privacy, sensitive-data handling, hallucinations, and appropriate safeguards.
MVP & Technical Approach — Determine what should realistically be built in 3 months using your PERN stack, without TypeScript or class constructors, while keeping the tools and services free.
Feature & Product Strategy — Separate essential MVP features from future features and identify what could make the app meaningfully different from existing wellness apps.

Sources to Check:

Official websites and documentation from existing wellness apps
App Store / Google Play information and user reviews
Academic and behavioral-health research where relevant
Industry reports and reputable market research
Official AI/API documentation and pricing
Developer documentation and credible technical sources
Community discussions such as Reddit, clearly separated from evidence-based research

Expected Deliverables:

Market/User Validation — evidence for whether the problem is useful to solve
Competitor Comparison Table — successful similar apps and their important features
Recommended Feature Set — MVP vs future features
AI Feature Analysis — what AI should analyze, how insights should be presented, and what safeguards are needed
Technical Recommendation — practical PERN architecture and free/low-cost tools
3-Month Development Roadmap
Cost/Budget Breakdown
Identified Product Opportunities/Gaps

The research should distinguish evidence, user sentiment, and researcher/model interpretation, rather than assuming that an idea is valuable simply because similar apps exist.
